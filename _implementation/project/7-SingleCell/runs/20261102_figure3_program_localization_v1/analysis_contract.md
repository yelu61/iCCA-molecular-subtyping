# Prospective analysis contract — 2026-11-02

Question: identify the broad cellular sources of six Figure2 expression patterns and eleven complete subtype-associated WGCNA gene sets, then evaluate malignant-restricted localization only after separate identity review. No C1/C2/C3 cell or patient labels are assigned from these scores.

Input: immutable 34,227-cell two-cohort broad atlas. Fixed full-member sets from Figure2 candidate manifest; no outcome-dependent selection. The 17 sets are evaluated in full, with current bulk labels retained only as source context.

Primary cell score: mean centered within-cell gene percentile rank, using all genes actually supplied by that library, average ranks for ties. Formula mean_g[(rank(x_g) − (G+1)/2)/(G−1)]. Its scale is approximately −0.5 to 0.5; it is not a bulk eigengene, a probability, a pathway activation assay, or a classification. Native unavailable genes are not expression zeros. No integrated expression is used.

Primary support: at least 70% of the defined members and 10 supplied members; at least 20 broad-eligible cells per library × compartment. Low-support libraries C46a/C52 remain in display/composition but do not enter expression inference. Library medians are averaged equally within patient × tissue × compartment; all patient estimates receive equal weight. Adjacent remains separate. Each patient's centered localization subtracts the equal-compartment mean of at least two supported compartments; its comparator composition is recorded. It is descriptive within-patient localization, not causal enrichment. Main cohort summaries remain separate.

Sensitivity fixed before scoring: (1) core members, pattern affinity≥0.6 or bulk kME≥0.5; (2) removal of all reviewed broad-annotation panel genes (includes liver-secreted marker candidates; not a substitute for ambient correction); (3) signature and full rank universe common to cohort × tissue libraries, retaining coverage requirements; (4) expression-matched control score: 20 bins of library mean log expression, all candidate-set genes excluded from control pool, up to 50 deterministic controls per bin, bin-control means weighted by target-gene fractions; (5) coverage 60%/80% and cell support 50; (6) pooled-cell instead of equal-library patient summaries. No threshold is optimized to obtain a preferred result.

Statistics: raw and centered patient summaries, 2,000 patient bootstrap percentile 95% intervals only when n≥4, otherwise descriptive point estimates with n. Predefined epithelial-minus-each-other-compartment paired contrasts use supported same-patient/same-tissue values. Exact two-sided sign-flip P values (n≥4) are exploratory; BH correction across all estimable 17-program × comparison tests within cohort × tissue. Intervals conditional on observed scoring/selection, with no full redevelopment uncertainty. Two tumor-adjacent paired patients are descriptive only. No cell-level inferential P values.

Plot contract: independent R-generated vector PDFs without panel letters, editable embedded fonts; R previews for inspection. Atlas display retains all cells and Uncertain. Composition denominators retain Uncertain; source proportions are captured-cell proportions, not tissue absolute abundance. Main localization is tumor-only with cohort facets and effective n. Native coverage, patient-level distributions, annotation-marker overlap, scoring/support sensitivity and adjacent observations are supplementary. No full manuscript Figure3 release until evidence-specific checks pass.

Implementation clarification before interpreting results: the enumerated sign-flip null assumes symmetry of patient differences; it is not a randomization test from randomized treatment assignment. Marker removal covers the predefined broad PANELS dictionary, not every adjudication marker or reference-model feature, and is not zero-overlap validation. The rank score can retain depth/detection dependence; within-library/compartment score-depth diagnostics are therefore required.
